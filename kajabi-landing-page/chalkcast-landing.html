How it works

Three steps from assignment to gradebook

  1. You set it upType the problem, add your rubric, and pick a time limit. chalkcast gives you a link to post in your course.
  2. Students teach itThey open the link, draw on the whiteboard, and explain out loud on camera.
  3. You grade itStudents upload the video and whiteboard PDF to your course site, such as Canvas, and you score them with your rubric.

Questions

What instructors ask first

Who is chalkcast for?

Instructors who ask students to explain how they got an answer. It was built for college anatomy and physiology, and it also fits nursing, chemistry, math, and high school science.

How do students submit assignments?

When they finish recording, students download a video and a PDF of their whiteboard and upload both to your course site, such as Canvas. chalkcast has no student accounts and stores nothing, so the files stay on the student's own device until they upload them.

Do students need an account or an app?

No. They click the link in your assignment and record in the browser on a computer with a camera and a microphone.

What is in the demo?

A sample problem, the whiteboard, and a one-minute recording. It is the same screen your students would use.
