# chalkcast privacy statement

Date: November 21, 2026

## The short version

A student's video, voice, camera image and whiteboard never leave their own device until the student uploads the files to their own college's learning system. chalkcast has no server, no accounts and no tracking.

## What chalkcast does with student work

- **Recording happens inside the browser.** The video and the whiteboard PDF are made on the student's device.
- **Nothing is uploaded by chalkcast.** The student downloads two files and uploads them to their own assignment.
- **No accounts.** Students do not sign in, and chalkcast does not ask for a name, email or student ID.
- **No tracking.** There are no cookies, analytics or advertising tools.

## What is saved on the student's device

So that a closed tab or a crash does not lose work, chalkcast keeps the whiteboard and the recording in the browser's own storage on that device.

- It stays on that device and in that browser only.
- It is erased when the student reaches the final upload screen, chooses "Start over", or starts a new problem.
- Anything left behind is erased the next time the page is opened if it is more than 24 hours old.
- On a shared or lab computer, a student who leaves partway through should reopen the link and choose "Start over", or clear the browser's site data.

## What is saved on an instructor's device

Instructor setup remembers the instructor's own assignment names, problems and rubrics in that browser so they can come back to them. It holds no student information.

## What travels in a student link

The link an instructor shares contains the problem, the rubric items and the time limit. It contains nothing about any student.

## Outside services

- **Hosting.** The page is a single file served by a web host (currently GitHub Pages). Like any web host, it can see that a visit happened and from what internet address. It does not receive recordings.
- **Fonts and code.** Everything the page needs is built into the file. It does not load fonts, scripts or images from any other service.

## After the student uploads

Once the files are in the college's learning system, they are covered by that college's own privacy rules and student record policies.

## Camera and microphone

chalkcast asks the browser for the camera and microphone only when the student presses "Turn on camera and mic". The browser shows its own permission prompt, and the student can turn access off at any time in the browser's site settings.
