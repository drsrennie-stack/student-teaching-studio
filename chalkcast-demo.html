02

Now record your own

The demo opens with a heart question and a rubric already loaded. You record for up to one minute, just as a student would.

  • Use a computer with a camera and a microphone, in Chrome, Edge, or Safari.
  • There is no account to create and nothing to install.
  • Your recording stays on your own device.
