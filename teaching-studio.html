      Assignment defense

      Ask one student a question about work they already turned in. The question stays hidden until the student presses Begin Defense, and recording starts at the same moment.

      Many defense links at once

      Each line becomes its own link with its own one-attempt record. The links use the title, recording time, due date, points, and whiteboard choice above.

      Nothing you type in this section is saved in the browser, so copy what you need before you leave. The question is packed into the link so it cannot be read at a glance, but it is not locked on a server, and a student with technical skill could unpack it. The one-attempt rule is kept on the student's own device. Server storage and server-side limits come with the sign-in version.
