**Three steps to your first assignment**
1. Open Instructor setup and fill in one box: a name, your problem, and a rubric if you want one.
2. Press "Make links and instructions".
3. Press "Copy instructions" and paste them into the assignment in your course.

**Before you assign it**
- Run through it once yourself as a student, so you know what they will see.
- Set the assignment to accept file uploads. If you use Canvas Studio for captions, allow more than one attempt.
- Press "Save my assignments to a file" when you finish. That file is your backup, and you can load it on another computer.
